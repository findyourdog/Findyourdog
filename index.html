Golden Retriever

Golden Retrievers are known for their friendly and gentle nature. They are highly intelligent and easy to train, making them one of the best family dogs. They love human interaction and are very loyal. This breed needs daily exercise and attention. They are also commonly used as therapy and service dogs due to their calm personality.

Shih Tzu

Shih Tzus are small indoor dogs originally bred for royalty. They are affectionate, calm, and enjoy being close to their owners. Despite their small size, they have a confident personality. Regular grooming is important due to their long coat. They are perfect for apartment living and love companionship.

Beagle

Beagles are energetic and extremely curious dogs. They have a strong sense of smell and love exploring their surroundings. This breed is friendly with children and other pets. They require regular walks and mental stimulation. Without activity, they can become bored and noisy.
