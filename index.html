Complete Puppy Care Guide

Everything you need to raise a perfect puppy

🍽️ Feeding

Feed 3–4 times daily with high-quality puppy food. Avoid harmful human food.

🛏️ Sleep

Puppies sleep 16–20 hours daily. Provide a safe and quiet space.

🚽 Training

Be consistent, use rewards, and never punish harshly.

🧠 Behavior

Teach basic commands early: sit, stay, come.

🐕 Socialization

Introduce your puppy to new people and environments early.

❤️ Health

Regular vet visits, vaccinations, and grooming are essential.
